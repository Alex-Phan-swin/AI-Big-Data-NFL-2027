🏈 NFL Big Data Bowl 2027 – Combine to NFL Performance

Project Overview

This project investigates whether movement patterns captured during the NFL Scouting Combine can help predict a player's performance in actual NFL games.

Using player-level sensor tracking data collected during Combine route-running drills and NFL regular-season games, we aim to identify movement characteristics that translate into on-field performance.

Our primary focus is Wide Receivers (WRs), investigating the relationship between their Combine movement mechanics and their ability to create separation from defenders during NFL passing plays.

The project explores whether advanced movement features derived from sensor tracking data provide additional predictive value beyond traditional Combine measurements.

🎯 Research Objective

Can movement characteristics extracted from NFL Combine route-running drills predict a wide receiver's ability to create separation during NFL games?

Traditional Combine measurements provide useful information about an athlete's physical capabilities. However, they may not fully capture the movement mechanics required to perform specific football actions.

This project explores whether sensor-derived movement characteristics can provide additional insights into NFL player performance.

Key research questions:

Do acceleration and deceleration patterns during Combine drills correlate with in-game receiving performance?

Does change-of-direction ability influence a wide receiver's ability to create separation?

Which movement characteristics are most strongly associated with NFL performance?

Do sensor-derived movement features improve predictive performance compared with traditional Combine measurements?

Are particular Combine route-running drills more informative than others?
